## 3. ⚠️ System back pops the stack — the oldest open item, closed

Asked for as *"add swipe to comeback when you enter in another screen like a detail"*.

Every log since 2026-08-21 carried this: **"Android's system back still exits the app
rather than popping."** The cause was structural. `App.kt` keeps its own back stack —
there is no navigation library — and Android's back went to the activity, which knew
nothing about it. So the gesture people reach for first threw away what they were doing,
and every screen had exactly one way out: the arrow in the app bar.

```kotlin
BackHandler(enabled = backStack.size > 1) { pop() }
```

- **`enabled` matters.** At a tab root, back must still leave the app. Intercepting it
  there would make the app impossible to exit by ordinary means.
- **It calls the same `pop()` as the arrow.** Two ways out running different code would
  drift, and the arrow is the one with coverage.
- **`ui-backhandler` is a separate artifact.** Not transitive from `compose-ui`;
  `implementation(libs.compose.ui.backhandler)` in `commonMain`.
- **It is experimental in CMP 1.11**, and the opt-in says why: the multiplatform
  contract around a "back" that iOS expresses as an edge swipe and Android as a gesture
  or a button is still settling. On Android it compiles to `OnBackPressedDispatcher`,
  which is not experimental.
- **No manifest change.** `targetSdk` is 36, where predictive back is already the
  default, so the gesture gets its animation without `enableOnBackInvokedCallback`.

Verified three ways on the emulator: the back *button* pops, a real left-edge swipe
pops, and back at a tab root still returns to the launcher.

**This is a common-code fix, so iOS gets it too** — and iOS has no system back at all,
which made the app-bar arrow the only exit there. That half is still unverified, like
everything else on that platform.

## 4. The week strip stops at today

Asked for as *"should not show future dates—only the current date and past dates, for
all features"*.

A training log records what happened. A cell for next Thursday invites a session on a
day that has not occurred, and every frequency, streak and "untrained this week" figure
then counts a workout nobody did. (The Log tab allowing a future session has been an
open item since 2026-08-21 too; this closes the *entry point*, not the underlying
guard.)

Three parts, all in `WeekStrip`, which is why "for all features" needed no work — the
Log and Weight tabs share the component:

- **The cells filter to `day <= today`.** A filter on the tail, not a change to
  `weekOf()`: a past week still draws seven. The survivors keep `weight(1f)`, so
  Saturday's six cells are *wider* rather than leaving a hole where Sunday was.
- **The forward arrow is disabled on the week holding today.** An arrow that empties
  the strip is worse than one that does not move.
- **The date picker greys out future days and years.** Otherwise the calendar is a way
  round the rule, landing on a day the strip then refuses to draw. Both halves are
  needed — without `isSelectableYear`, picking 2031 moves the grid to a month where
  every cell is disabled.

## 5. Two small ones

**Cancel to the left, Go to the right** in the date picker. Material puts both at the
end, which reads as a pair of equals and makes leaving the neighbour of the thing you
usually want. Implemented by putting both buttons in `confirmButton` as one
`SpaceBetween` row and leaving `dismissButton` null — filling it would add a third
button after the row.

**The selected tab's label is semi-bold.** The bar already tints the selection and puts
a pill behind its icon, but both of those are colour, and colour alone is the one signal
that does not survive greyscale, a colour-vision difference, or bright sunlight. The
weight says the same thing in a second channel.
